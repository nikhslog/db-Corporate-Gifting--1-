  Our story

We believe in the quiet gift.

The kind that's chosen, not ordered. That carries a brand lightly. That someone keeps on their desk for years and still remembers who it came from.

i.

Chosen, not ordered

Every brief starts with the people receiving the gift: who they are, what they'd actually use, what would feel personal. We shortlist from makers and brands we've handled ourselves, so nothing arrives as a surprise.

ii.

Branded with restraint

Your logo belongs on the gift, but it shouldn't shout. Foil on a lid, an engraving inside a folio, a card in your colours. We show you a mockup of every piece before anything is produced.

iii.

Delivered like it matters

Wrapped by hand, packed to arrive the way it left us, and tracked to every address. One person looks after your order from the first conversation to the last delivery.

What we believe

Three principles

Fewer, better things

A smaller catalogue we can stand behind, rather than thousands of items we've never touched.

The recipient first

Gifts people use and keep. If it would end up in a drawer, it doesn't go in the box.

Care you can see

Paper, ribbon, finish and the note inside. Presentation is part of the gift.

The people behind db

A note from the founder

db began at a kitchen table one Diwali, packing forty boxes for a friend's company. Halfway through, we realised nobody had asked who the gifts were actually for. Every box we've made since has started with that question.

We still read every brief ourselves, still sample every new piece before it joins the catalogue, and still believe the best corporate gift is the one nobody throws away.

The founder, db
